FAQ

How do I pay?

Payment happens outside the app, after the operator’s call. They’ll tell you the amount and payment method.

How much does it cost?

The seller’s ticket price plus a 7% service fee. You see the total before sending the request.

How are tickets checked?

Sellers upload a ticket photo for the moderator, and the operator checks the ticket on every request before payment.

Do I need to confirm my number by SMS?

No. To sign up you only need a name, phone number and role — buyer or seller.

What if the event is cancelled?

The organiser is responsible for holding, rescheduling or cancelling the event. See the Terms of Use for details.
